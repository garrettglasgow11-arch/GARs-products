Pocket Colony

An unofficial, fan-made ant colony simulator built for the browser. Forage the field, keep the queen fed, dig out new chambers and raid rival bugs for loot.

How to play

Field — your workers forage on their own. Tap the ground to drop a pheromone trail and they'll swarm that spot. Soldiers patrol the mound and fight off intruders.

Nest — the queen turns leaves into eggs. Hatch eggs into workers (gatherers) or soldiers (fighters), and spend sand on chambers to raise your caps.

Raid — send soldiers at rival camps for loot. Wounded soldiers die for good, so bring enough.

Progress saves to this browser automatically.

Fan project

A fan game inspired by mobile ant-colony sims — built from scratch, with original code and art. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or containing assets from any commercial game or its publisher.

Free, no ads, no tracking, no accounts. Runs fully offline once loaded.
